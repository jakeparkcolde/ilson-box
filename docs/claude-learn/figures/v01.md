# Figure briefs — v01 손님 동선부터 그려 보기 (심화 1강)

Common rules: SPEC §8a and `FIGURE-STYLE.md` (SVG only, viewBox width 720, height ≤ 480, light plate `#f5f6f6`, palette tokens, Korean font stack, ≤ ~12 words per label, no logos, no brand marks of GitHub, Supabase or Vercel — service names are plain text in the Korean font). Every text at least 18px at the 720 design width (main labels 20–24px, headings up to 26px). Placeholders are never drawn: use the example shop 「햇살꽃집」 and its product 「계절 꽃다발」 only where a shop name is needed. No prices inside figures except where noted. **Keep a clear zone of about 120 × 72 px in the bottom-right corner of every figure (zoom badge).**

Colour meaning in this lesson: purple = the customer's path / what the reader decides; green = what we collect or build; red = what we never collect or show; amber = 「[확인 필요]」 or a paid-plan note.
