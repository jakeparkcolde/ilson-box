The lesson's image is the Korean proverb 「돌다리도 두들겨 보고 건너라」 (tap even a stone bridge before you cross). The three habits are three stepping stones, always in this order: **근거 묻기 → 확인·추측 가르기 → 숫자 다시 계산**.
