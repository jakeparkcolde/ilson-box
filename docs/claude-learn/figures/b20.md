Common rules: SPEC §8a and `FIGURE-STYLE.md` (SVG only, viewBox width 720, height ≤ 480, light plate `#f5f6f6`, palette tokens, Korean font stack, ≤ ~12 words per label, no logos). Text size: at least 18px at the 720 design width (main labels 20–24px, cover heading up to 26px). Placeholders are never drawn; use 「햇살꽃집」 or neutral words. No numbers, percentages or prices inside the figures except 「5시간」 and the plan names; bars are drawn partly filled with no value.

**Keep the bottom-right corner free**: leave an empty area of about 150 × 64 px in the bottom-right of every plate (no text, cards or arrows there). The screen-sketch note 「실제 화면은 조금 다를 수 있어요」 goes bottom-left instead.

Colour meaning in this lesson: purple = where to look / what you do; amber = getting close to a limit (careful); green = the calm default (wait); white = neutral.
